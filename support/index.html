Before you contact us

Live timing is available only while a supported session is active. Between sessions, the app may correctly show no live data.

These quick steps resolve most connection and display problems:

  • Check that your device has a working internet connection.
  • Close and reopen FormulaVision.
  • Open Settings and try the alternative live-data source.
  • Install the latest available version of the app.

Common questions

Why is live timing not showing?

Live information appears only during supported race-weekend sessions. If a session is active, confirm your internet connection, restart the app, and try the alternative data source in Settings.

Why do the schedule or standings look outdated?

The schedule and standings depend on public data services and can occasionally update later than the live event. Reopen the screen to request fresh data and check again after a few minutes.

Why is a circuit map or detail missing?

Some venues and newly announced events may not have complete map or session data yet. Include the event name in your support email so we can investigate the specific entry.

Does the app require an account?

No. FormulaVision does not require an account. Your settings are stored locally on your device. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include:

  • Your device model and operating-system version
  • The FormulaVision app version
  • The screen or event where the issue occurred
  • A short description of what you expected and what happened
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem
