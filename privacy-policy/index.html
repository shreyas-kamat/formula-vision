FormulaVision

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 June 2026

FormulaVision is an unofficial motorsport live-timing companion app. We designed it to be private by default: you do not need to create an account, and the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

FormulaVision does not require an account and does not ask for, gather, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, or other personal files
  • Payment or financial information

We have no servers that store information about you, and we have no way to identify individual users.

Data Stored on Your Device

To remember your choices, the app saves a small set of preferences locally on your device — for example your preferred speed unit, the track-map display setting, the race-control sound toggle, the selected data-feed source, and a cache of recent championship standings.

This information stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

To show live timing and race data, FormulaVision connects over the internet to services that we do not own or operate:

  • Official motorsport timing-data providers, for live session and timing data
  • Public motorsport data APIs, for results, schedules, and standings
  • Media content delivery services, for circuit, driver, and team imagery

As with any internet request, these services may receive standard technical connection information (such as your IP address) as a normal part of delivering content to your device. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

FormulaVision is an unofficial application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any motorsport championship, series, governing body, or team. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

App Permissions

On Android, the app requests a single permission — internet access (INTERNET) — which is required to fetch live race data. On iOS, the app does not request any privacy-sensitive permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or similar).

Analytics, Advertising & Tracking

FormulaVision contains none of the following:

  • No analytics or usage-tracking tools
  • No crash-reporting services
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No cookies set by us
  • No third-party trackers or device fingerprinting

Push Notifications

The app does not send push notifications and contains no notification or messaging services.

Data Security

Because the app collects no personal data, there is no personal information for us to expose or lose. Connections to the data services listed above use secure, encrypted transport (HTTPS / WSS) where provided by those services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" shown at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
